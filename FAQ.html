❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding academic and campus services.

🎓 Admission FAQ

Q1. What is the eligibility for course registration?

Students enrolled in university degree programs with valid Student IDs are eligible.

Q2. How do I track my admission application?

You can log into the portal and check the Application Status under your Student Dashboard.

💳 Fees FAQ

Q1. What payment methods are supported for term fees?

Net Banking, UPI, Debit/Credit Cards, and Demand Drafts at the accounts section.

Q2. Where can I download my fee receipt?

Fee receipts are generated automatically under the 'Transactions' tab in your Profile.

🏢 Hostel FAQ

Q1. How do I apply for campus hostel allotment?

Hostel registration forms open at the start of each academic semester in the portal.

Q2. What are the hostel curfew timings?

In-campus movement is allowed till 09:30 PM with valid ID verification.

💻 Technical FAQ

Q1. What if I forget my Student Portal password?

Click on 'Forgot Password' on the Login page or update it from Settings.

Q2. Assignment file upload is failing. What should I do?

Ensure your file format is PDF/DOCX and size is under 10MB before re-submitting.
