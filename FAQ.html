❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding academic and campus services.

🎓 Admission FAQ

Students enrolled in university degree programs with valid Student IDs are eligible for registering for various course tracks.
You can log into the portal and check the Application Status under your Student Dashboard in real-time.

💳 Fees FAQ

We support Net Banking, UPI, Debit/Credit Cards, and Demand Drafts submitted directly at the campus accounts section.
Fee receipts are generated automatically under the 'Transactions' tab in your Profile after successful payment.

🏢 Hostel FAQ

Hostel registration forms open at the start of each academic semester through the Student Hub portal.
In-campus movement is allowed till 09:30 PM with valid student ID card verification at the main hostel desk.

💻 Technical FAQ

You can click on 'Forgot Password' on the Login page or update/reset your password directly from the Settings section.
Ensure your file format is PDF/DOCX and the file size is under 10MB before re-submitting. Clear browser cache if issue persists.
